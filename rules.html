Part One — Definitions

These terms carry the same meaning throughout.

Card
One performance — a single session at the fronton, either the 1:00pm matinee or the 7:00pm evening card.
Game
One match within a card. Nine games per card, ten on Saturday evenings.
Season
All 99 cards between January 5 and March 31, 2027. 911 games are played; 903 of them count toward these contests.
Contest Period
The 67 cards between February 2 and March 31, 2027, during which Chula League scoring occurs.
Study Period
The 24 cards between January 5 and January 23, 2027, used to set athlete salaries. Results count toward Club League standings but not toward Chula League scoring.
Entry
One submitted, verified and (where applicable) paid registration.
Post
One of the eight positions, numbered 1 through 8, from which entries compete in a game.
Entry Points
The score awarded to a single post in a single game, calculated as set out in Part Four.
Official Results
The results published by the Administrator following receipt of the venue's results feed.
Administrator
Fantasy League Jai Alai Club, which operates all three contests.

Part Two — The Club League

Free to enter. No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or to win.

2.1 Eligibility

Open to natural persons aged 18 or over at the time of entry who are legal residents of the United States. Employees, contractors and immediate family members of the Administrator, of Dania Beach Jai Alai, and of any sponsor of this contest are not eligible. Athletes on the 2027 roster and fronton officials are not eligible. Void where prohibited.

2.2 How to enter

One entry per person. Entries require a club selection from the eight clubs, plus three predictions for that club across the season, each a whole number between 60 and 160: a win total (1st place finishes), a place total (2nd place finishes) and a show total (3rd place finishes).

Only the win total decides the title. Places and shows are used solely to break a tie, in that order.

There is no limit on how many entrants may select the same club. Selecting a popular club neither improves nor reduces an entrant's chance of winning.

Entries must be verified by email link. An entry is not active until the link is clicked. Duplicate entries by the same person will be reduced to the first verified entry.

Entry period: opens on publication of these rules and closes at 11:59pm ET on January 4, 2027.

2.3 How clubs are assigned

Each club is assigned one post for the whole of each card. Assignments advance by one post per card and by one additional post at the start of each week. The complete assignment for all 99 cards is published in advance and does not change. Across the season every club is assigned to every post between 109 and 118 times. No club holds an advantaged position.

2.4 How wins are counted

A club is credited with a win when the post it holds wins a game. Where a game goes to post with fewer than eight entries, the unused posts are not credited and are not penalised; because assignments rotate, every club is affected equally over the season.

The winning club is the one credited with the most wins across all 903 scoring games. If two or more clubs tie on wins, the tie is broken by total points scored, then by wins in games decided in the second round of scoring.

2.5 Club League Champion — $7,000 and the Championship Belt

Awarded to the entrant, among those who selected the winning club, whose predicted win total is closest to that club's actual win total. Overestimates and underestimates are treated equally.

Where two or more entrants are equally close, the tie is broken by closest predicted place total, then closest predicted show total. If entrants remain tied after all three, the cash is divided equally and each tied entrant receives a Championship Belt.

Every club is expected to finish near 113 wins, 113 places and 113 shows. The club that wins the season typically records around 130 wins, and slightly below average places — a game it wins cannot also be a game it places in.

2.6 Biggest Fan — $3,000 and the Biggest Fan Championship Belt

Awarded to the entrant with the highest fan points total across the whole field. Open to every entrant regardless of which club they selected or how that club performed.

Part Three — The Player League

Free to enter. No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or to win.

3.1 Eligibility

As 2.1 above.

3.2 How to enter

One entry per person, verified by email link. The contest begins with the first card on Tuesday January 5, 2027 and runs to the last card on March 31.

Each entry is six different players from the 22-man roster, with two assigned to win, two to place and two to show. There is no salary cap and no position requirement — any six may be chosen.

3.3 Scoring

Scoring runs in every game on every scoring card of the season. Points count only for the role assigned — a win-role player finishing second scores nothing.

Doubles. Both players in a doubles entry are credited with that entry's finish, so a player paired with a stronger partner takes the full result. Absences. A player who does not appear simply has fewer opportunities; there is no substitution.

3.4 Changes

At each of three windows an entrant may replace up to two of their six players and reassign all roles freely. Role reassignment does not count against the two-player limit.

3.5 Prizes

Highest season total wins $15,000 and the Championship Belt, funded by the contest sponsor. Ties break on most win-role finishes, then place, then show; if entrants remain tied the cash divides equally and each receives a belt.

Part Four — The Chula League

Entry fee $100. A contest of skill. Outcomes are determined predominantly by entrants' knowledge and judgement of athlete performance.

4.1 Eligibility

As 2.1 above, with the addition that entrants must be legal residents of a state in which participation in paid fantasy sports contests is permitted. The list of eligible states is published on the entry page and forms part of these rules.

4.2 Entry

Prices publish Monday, January 25, 2027. Entries close 11:59pm ET, Saturday, January 30, 2027.

Each entry consists of six athletes selected from the 22-player roster, subject to a minimum of two frontcourt athletes, a minimum of two backcourt athletes, a total salary at or under the published cap, and one athlete designated captain who scores 1.5× their Entry Points.

Each athlete carries a published salary derived from Study Period results. Salaries are fixed at publication and do not change thereafter. Results between January 25 and January 30 do not alter published salaries.

A maximum of three entries per person. Each requires a separate fee and a separate lineup. Entries beyond the third from the same person, household or payment method will be refunded and voided.

4.3 Lineup changes

Lineups lock at the close of entries and remain locked, with two exceptions. Each entry may change one athlete at each of the two segment breaks:

  • Swap window 1 — opens after the final card of February 20, closes before the first card of February 23
  • Swap window 2 — opens after the final card of March 13, closes before the first card of March 16

Swaps require the resulting lineup to satisfy all constraints above, including the salary cap at published salaries. No reason need be given. Swaps are optional and do not carry over.

No other changes are permitted for any reason, including an athlete's absence, injury, or removal from the roster. An athlete who does not appear scores no points and is not replaced outside a swap window.

4.6 Payment and tax

Entry fees are collected through a third-party payment processor. Entry fees are non-refundable once an entry is verified and paid, except where a contest is cancelled under 5.4.

No taxpayer identification is collected at entry. Where an entrant's total prizes reach $600 or more for the season, a Form W-9 will be requested before payment and a Form 1099 issued as required. Prizes are the recipient's income and all taxes are the recipient's responsibility.

Part Five — Scoring

These rules apply to all three contests.

5.1 Entry Points

FinishPoints
Win (1st)10
Place (2nd)6
Show (3rd)3
Fourth or lower0

Scoring is on finishing position only. The venue publishes the win, place and show finishers for each game and does not publish the points scored on the board, so no points are awarded for scoring within a game.

Games are played either to seven points (“Spectacular 7”) or to nine (“Spectacular 9”) as set by the venue. Because scoring is finish-only, game length does not affect an entry’s score. Some games record a fourth finisher for superfecta wagering; fourth place scores nothing.

5.2 Application

Club League: a club receives the Entry Points of the post it held, once per game.

Chula League: each athlete receives the Entry Points of the entry they appeared in. In doubles, both partners receive the full Entry Points — the score is not divided. The captain multiplier of 1.5× is applied after Entry Points are totalled. All games count, singles and doubles alike.

5.3 Games that do not score

The venue runs a separate bracket series on eight Friday evenings — February 5, 12, 19 and 26, and March 5, 12, 19 and 26 — contested between two teams rather than the usual eight posts. Bracket matches are excluded from all three contests. They are not scored and do not count toward any club's totals. 911 games are played across the season; 903 of them score.

5.4 Short fields

Where a game goes to post with fewer than eight entries, only the posts that competed are scored. Absent posts receive no points and no penalty.

Part Six — Results and Cancellation

6.1 Source of results

Official Results derive from the results feed provided by Dania Beach Jai Alai. Where the feed and the Administrator's published standings conflict, the venue's feed governs.

6.2 Timing

Results are published the day after each card. Both the matinee and the evening card of a given day are published together on the following day. There is no live in-game scoring and standings do not update during play.

6.3 Corrections

The Administrator may correct published standings to reflect an error in the feed or in processing. Corrections may be made up to 72 hours after a period closes, after which standings for that period are final. A correction that changes a prize outcome will be published with an explanation.

6.4 Cancellation and shortened seasons

If a card is cancelled or postponed it is simply not scored. Scoring periods retain their published dates and are not extended.

If the season is shortened such that fewer than 50% of Contest Period cards are played, the Administrator will either extend the Contest Period, declare standings final as at the last completed period, or cancel the Chula League and refund entry fees in full. The Club League, being free to enter, will be settled on results as played.

Part Seven — General

7.1 Notification and claims

Winners are notified by the email address on their entry within 14 days of the relevant period closing. A winner who does not respond within 30 days forfeits the prize, which may be awarded to the next-placed entrant.

7.2 Identity

The Administrator may require proof of identity, age and residence before paying a prize. Entries containing false information are void.

7.3 Conduct

Any attempt to manipulate results, submit entries by automated means, or interfere with another entrant's participation voids all of that person's entries.

7.4 Changes to these rules

These rules may be amended before entries open. After entries open, no amendment may alter scoring, prize structure or eligibility except where required by law or to correct a manifest error.

Part Eight — Legal Notices

No purchase necessary — Club League

NO PURCHASE, PAYMENT OR CONTRIBUTION OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR TO WIN THE CLUB LEAGUE. A purchase or contribution will not improve your chance of winning. The Club League is void where prohibited or restricted by law.

The Chula League is a contest of skill. Outcomes reflect entrants' relative knowledge and judgement of athlete performance, exercised through roster selection under a salary cap. Winning is not determined by chance. The Chula League is void where prohibited or restricted by law, and is offered only to residents of states listed on the entry page.

Eligibility and age

Entrants must be 18 years of age or older. Where a state or jurisdiction sets a higher minimum age for participation in paid contests, that higher age applies. The Administrator may require proof of age and will void any entry that cannot be verified.

Taxes

Prizes are income to the recipient. All federal, state and local taxes are the sole responsibility of the winner. Where total prizes to an individual reach $600 or more in a calendar year, the Administrator will request a Form W-9 before payment and will issue a Form 1099 as required by law. Personal information is collected solely to administer the contests, verify eligibility and pay prizes. It is not sold and is not shared other than with service providers acting on the Administrator's behalf, or where required by law. Payment card details are handled by the payment processor and are never stored by the Administrator. Entrants may request deletion of their information after the season concludes and after any tax reporting obligations are satisfied.

The Administrator is not liable for network, hardware or software failures; for errors, delays or omissions in the results feed supplied by the venue; for lost, late, incomplete or misdirected entries; or for events beyond its reasonable control that prevent the season being completed as scheduled. The Administrator's total liability to any entrant is limited to the amount of that entrant's entry fee. The Club League being free to enter, no liability attaches in respect of it.

Disputes

Decisions of the Administrator on all matters relating to these contests are final and binding, subject to the correction window in 5.3. Any dispute is governed by the laws of the State of Florida without regard to conflict-of-law principles, and the parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Broward County, Florida.

Responsible play

Play within your means. Entry fees should never exceed what you can comfortably afford to lose. If participation stops being enjoyable, stop. Confidential help is available 24 hours a day from the National Problem Gambling Helpline at 1-800-522-4700, and in Florida from 1-888-ADMIT-IT. The Administrator will close the account of any entrant who requests self-exclusion and will refund unentered balances.

Severability

If any provision of these rules is held invalid or unenforceable, that provision is severed and the remaining provisions continue in full force.
