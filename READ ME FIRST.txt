PRIZES - MINIMUM $35,000
-------------------------
  ROAD TO STATE CHAMPS            $5,000   OPEN NOW
      $2,500 winner + $2,500 winner's school
  CLASS CHAMPIONSHIPS             $5,000   2027
      $2,500 winner + $2,500 winner's school
  UNDISPUTED FLORIDA STATE CHAMPS $20,000  2027
      $10,000 cash to one winner
       $5,000 cash donation to the winner's school
       $5,000 cash donation to Florida's No.1 school (MaxPreps)
  THE DRAFT              $2,500 min per Group, 2 Groups min   2027
      50/50 - half to that Group's winner, half to the school

Three of the four are statewide pots ANY Florida student can win
from - $30,000, and entering one does not use up a chance at the
others. The Draft is the exception: sixteen students at one school
competing only against each other.

Not on the site but in rule 5.5: if Florida's No.1 school is EXCLUDED
from the league, its $5,000 goes to the winner's school instead, so
that school receives $10,000. The winner's own $10,000 is never
affected. Worth repeating in whatever you send winners.

Arithmetic note: $10k + $5k + $5k is $20,000, not $25,000.


TWO THINGS TO CLOSE BEFORE LAUNCH
----------------------------------
1. MAXPREPS. Rule 5.2 settles Undisputed on the final MaxPreps
   Florida rankings, and MaxPreps is named in four places on the
   site. Reading a published ranking is fine; making a third party's
   editorial product the settlement mechanism for a $20,000 prize
   and putting their name across your site is the part to clear.
   ASK THEM BEFORE LAUNCH.

   [NAMED ALTERNATE SOURCE] in rule 5.2 is still a placeholder. If
   MaxPreps stops publishing a Florida top ten, that clause is the
   only thing stopping the game voiding.

2. TRADEMARK. Reg. No. 4005322, FRIDAY NIGHT LIGHTS, held by Orange
   County Youth Sports Association, covers amateur youth sports
   services and youth football programs. NBCUniversal owns the
   entertainment side. Clear the name with a trademark attorney
   before printing merch, filing an application, or signing a
   franchisee.


WHO CAN ENTER
--------------
Students enrolled at a Florida public, private or charter high school
who are legal residents of Florida. No parents, staff, alumni or
community members. Under-18 needs guardian approval before the entry
activates. Rostered football players, coaches and administrators
cannot enter.


EXCLUDED SCHOOLS - TWO DIFFERENT THINGS
----------------------------------------
Currently excluded:  NSU University School (Fort Lauderdale)
That is the school in Davie. "Nova (Davie)" in 6A is DIFFERENT and is
not excluded.

Excluding a school bars its STUDENTS from entering and stops it
receiving a school share. It does NOT remove the school as a team -
it stays fully selectable in every game.

Add more to EXCLUDED_SCHOOLS in the CONFIG block of index.html.
Leave rule 2.4's wording alone; it states exclusion is not a finding
of wrongdoing and gives a school a route to ask for removal.

THE EQUITY ARGUMENT (Section 5 of the guide)
---------------------------------------------
The two $5,000 shares in Undisputed are INDEPENDENT:

  team finishes No.1, no student wins ......... $5,000
  a student wins, team nowhere near No.1 ...... $5,000
  both ........................................ $10,000
  student wins + No.1 school excluded ......... $10,000
  school has no football programme at all ..... $5,000

The line for a district or school board: a school with no football
money and no team at all can still send $5,000 back to its building
because one of its students read the state better than anyone else
in Florida. Cost to the district is zero.
