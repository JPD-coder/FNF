GAME 1

The Club League

Free to enter · Unlimited entries · $10,000 in prizes

Pick a Club, Root All Season. Every game at Dania Beach Jai Alai has eight entries standing at posts 1 through 8. Each session, each club is assigned a post — whoever stands there all night is playing for your club. You don't need to know a single player's name. You just need to watch and root for your favorite team.

Results are posted the day after each card. Standings run on a one-day lag all season — nothing updates live during play.

How to enter

  • Pick your club. Any of the eight. No caps, no limits — if 800 people want the Square Groupers, that's 800 Square Groupers fans.
  • Predict wins, places and shows. Every club lands near 113 of each across the season. The club that wins the season usually records about 130 wins — and slightly fewer places than average, because a game it wins can't also be one it places in. Closest win count takes the title; places then shows break any tie.

It's a dead-even race, which means it's genuinely undecided in March. Before entering you'll be asked to read and accept the rules.

GAME 2

The Player League

Free to enter · Unlimited entries · $15,000 in prizes

Runs all season long. Pick six players from the 22-man roster. Then decide which two will win, which two will place, and which two will show. Hold them from opening night to the last card of the season — all 99 cards and 903 scoring games, starting January 5. No salary cap, no position minimum, no study period. Pick any six.

How it scores

Your players score every time they finish in the role you gave them — in every game, on every card, all season.

A win player finishes 1st9
A place player finishes 2nd6
A show player finishes 3rd3

Only the assigned role counts. Your win player finishing second earns nothing — which is the whole point.

Why reading beats reputation

Anyone can name the best player on the roster. The question this game asks is harder: who is undervalued at second and third?

Last season the roster's best show man ranked only tenth by win rate. Nobody picking on reputation would have found him. Somebody reading the numbers would have.

And seven of the 22 are brand new with no record anywhere. Getting a rookie right is the biggest edge on the board.

Two swaps a window, three windows

  • Feb 2, Feb 23 and Mar 16. At each you may change up to two of your six.
  • Roles are free to reassign at every window. Realised your show man is actually winning? Move him, and it costs you nothing.
  • Do nothing and nothing happens. Your six and their roles carry straight on.

GAME 3

The Chula League

$100 to enter · $85,000 in prizes · Feb 2 – Mar 31

Know Jai-Alai? Pick Six and Prove It. Show off your jai-alai knowledge and earn your bragging rights. Pick six players from Dania Beach Jai Alai's 22-man roster and ride them for two months. No weekly homework, no lineup to reset every Tuesday. Set it and watch.

Your lineup

  • Six players, minimum two frontcourt and minimum two backcourt.
  • Every player has a price and you have a fixed budget. You cannot afford the six best — that's the whole game.
  • Name one captain, who scores 1.5×.

How you score

Your players earn points all season from real results: win (1st), place (2nd) and show (3rd). Nothing below third. Every game counts, singles and doubles.

Results are posted the day after each card — Tuesday's matinee and evening both land on Wednesday. There is no live in-game scoring.

When you're handicapping, back rate over reputation. Last season every full-time player worked between 47% and 61% of the card — barely a third of a difference between the busiest and the quietest. Win rates spread more than three times as wide. Who performs matters far more than who gets scheduled.

Three weeks to do your homework

The season opens January 5 but nothing counts for the first three weeks. You watch, you study, and so does everyone else.

Seven of the 22 players are brand new this year with no history anywhere. By late January you'll have seen every one of them play roughly 120 times — enough to separate them, and the single biggest edge available to anyone who watches.

Who can enter

Aged 18 or over, legal residents of the United States. Employees and immediate family of the Administrator, of Dania Beach Jai Alai, and of any sponsor are not eligible, nor are athletes on the 2027 roster or fronton officials.

Your entry

One entry per person, verified by email link — your entry is not active until you click it. You pick one of the eight clubs and predict its wins (1st), places (2nd) and shows (3rd) across the season, each between 60 and 160. Only the win total decides the title; places then shows break a tie. Entries close 11:59pm ET on January 4, 2027.

How it is decided

Clubs rotate through all eight posts and each plays all 903 scoring games, so no club holds an advantage. The Club League Champion is whoever, among those who picked the winning club, predicted its win total closest — $7,000 and the Championship Belt. If entrants remain tied after places and shows, the cash divides equally and each receives a belt. The Biggest Fan is the highest fan-points total across the whole field regardless of club — $3,000 and the Biggest Fan Championship Belt.

Games that do not score

The venue runs a separate bracket series on eight Friday evenings in February and March. Those matches are two teams head to head rather than the usual eight posts, and they are excluded from all three games. 911 games are played across the season; 903 of them score.

Rules — please read
Free to enter

No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or to win. A purchase will not improve your chance of winning. Void where prohibited.

Who can enter

Aged 18 or over, legal residents of the United States. Employees and immediate family of the Administrator, of Dania Beach Jai Alai, and of any sponsor are not eligible, nor are athletes on the 2027 roster or fronton officials.

How you play

Pick six different players from the 22-man roster and assign two to win, two to place and two to show. There is no salary cap and no position requirement. Your entry runs from the first card on January 5 through the last on March 31.

Scoring

A player scores 9 every time they finish first while assigned to win, 6 every time they finish second while assigned to place, and 3 every time they finish third while assigned to show — in every game, on every card. Points count only for the role assigned; a win player finishing second scores nothing.

Changes

At each of three windows — February 2, February 23 and March 16 — you may replace up to two of your six players and reassign all roles freely. If you do nothing, your six and their roles carry on unchanged. No entry lapses.

Doubles and absences

Both players in a doubles entry are credited with that entry's finish, so a player paired with a stronger partner takes the full result. A player who does not appear simply has fewer opportunities; there is no substitution.

Prize

Highest season total wins $15,000 and the Championship Belt, funded by the contest sponsor. Ties break on most win-role finishes, then place, then show; if still tied the cash divides equally and each receives a belt.

Results and independence
